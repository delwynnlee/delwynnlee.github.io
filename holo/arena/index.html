Arena

Pick two decks, then play against the AI yourself or watch two AI players battle with real Pokémon cards. Every attack, Ability and Trainer is played by the rules of today's game.

Shuffling the card pool

Choose a deck

The rules engine plays 1,827 cards from the Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution series whose text it can read. Special Energy, Pokémon with rules it can't follow yet, and a few rare effects are left out.

Holo Arena is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Creatures or Game Freak. Card text comes from the open pokemon-tcg-data set and pictures from TCGdex and pokemontcg.io.

Learn to play

Seven short lessons, from your first Basic Pokémon to taking the last Prize card. Each one sets up a board and walks you through it, one tap at a time.
