Choose a deck

The rules engine plays 1,827 cards from the Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution series whose text it can read. Most Special Energy, Pokémon with rules it can't follow yet, and a few rare effects are left out.

Holo Arena is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Creatures or Game Freak. Card text comes from the open pokemon-tcg-data set and pictures from TCGdex and pokemontcg.io.

Decks

Claude's decks and the finalists' decks from the World Championships, with how each one plans to win. Open a deck to read its strategy and card list, then play it.

Learn to play

Seven short lessons, from your first Basic Pokémon to taking the last Prize card. Each one sets up a board and walks you through it, one tap at a time.

Famous finals

World Championship finals, with both finalists' decks exactly as they played them. Take either seat against the other deck: win the way the champion did, or win the game the runner-up couldn't.
